# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Seokwon Yoon, Youngbin Choi, Seunghyuk Cho, Seungbeom Lee,
MoonJeong Park, and Dongwoo Kim for publishing *Routing by Reaching:
Composition of Pre-trained GFlowNets for Multi-Objective Generation* and for
releasing the `gflownet-composition` implementation.

The paper's reaching-probability mixer gives a precise mechanism to audit: the
exact linear identity, the full neural HyperGrid comparison, the no-reaching
ablation, and the nonlinear state-level distortion can each be translated into
explicit producers and controls. The public source also makes it possible to
state honestly when the molecule claims cannot be reproduced because essential
checkpoints or comparator surfaces are absent.

This repository is an independent reproduction audit, not the authors'
implementation and not a replacement for the paper. The positive, falsified,
and blocked outcomes are preserved so future work can extend the missing
molecule routes without losing the current evidence boundary. Please see
[`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) when reusing the paper or this archive.
